## Rolling restart follow-up: APPROVE — implementation

The live rollout of `376aece` exposed stale authorization after a worker process
restart that remained healthy between probes. The additional full delta was
reviewed against that candidate, and the complete runtime diff was rechecked
against `ff38072`. No unresolved P1/P2 implementation findings remain.

Health now exposes the nonsecret process session identity. A detected change
excludes the worker until its handshake succeeds, including dispatches selected
before the change. Failed refresh retries on later healthy polls; older health
responses without a session identity preserve the compatibility behavior.

Two P1 concurrency findings in the initial follow-up were independently
reproduced and corrected before this sign-off:

1. A token minted before refresh could pass the pending check after refresh
   completed. The pending fence and current token mint now execute together
   under the pool lock after RPC client acquisition, immediately before POST.
   Network I/O remains outside the lock. Independent token validation confirmed
   that a dispatch waiting for its client uses the refreshed session.
2. A second restart between health probe and handshake could retain the first
   pending session indefinitely. Pending identity now follows later health
   observations even when the registered session already matches. Independent
   reproduction confirmed recovery on the next current-session probe.

Reported final focused worker-pool/server checks: **164 passed** in 7.17 seconds;
Ruff and whitespace checks passed. Regressions cover healthy process replacement,
failed-refresh dispatch fencing and retry, older health replies, client-acquisition
interleaving and restart during handshake. A worker restart after the local mint
boundary can still fail cleanly; no automatic replay or legacy downgrade is added.
